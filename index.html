    The Six Kalmas

    First Kalma (Tayyab)
    لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَّسُولُ اللَّهِ

    There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and Muhammad (peace be upon him) is the Messenger of Allah.

    Second Kalma (Shahadah)
    أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

    I bear witness that there is no deity except Allah, alone without partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger.

    Third Kalma (Tamjeed)
    سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَلَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ

    Glory be to Allah, all praise is for Allah, there is no deity except Allah, and Allah is the Greatest.

    Fourth Kalma (Tawheed)
    لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ وَهُوَ حَيٌّ لَا يَمُوتُ أَبَدًا أَبَدًا

    There is no deity except Allah alone; to Him belongs all dominion and praise, He gives life and causes death, and He is Ever-Living and never dies.

    Fifth Kalma (Astaghfar)
    أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ رَبِّي مِنْ كُلِّ ذَنْبٍ أَذْنَبْتُهُ عَمْدًا أَوْ خَطَأً سِرًّا أَوْ عَلَانِيَةً

    I seek forgiveness from Allah, my Lord, for every sin I committed intentionally or by mistake, in secret or in open.

    Sixth Kalma (Radd-e-Kufr)
    اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ أُشْرِكَ بِكَ شَيْئًا وَأَنَا أَعْلَمُ وَأَسْتَغْفِرُكَ لِمَا لَا أَعْلَمُ

    O Allah, I seek refuge in You from knowingly associating anything with You, and I seek Your forgiveness for what I do not know.

    Short Surahs & Special Recitations

    Surah Al-Ikhlas (112)
    قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

    Say, "He is Allah, [who is] One, Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, nor is there to Him any equivalent."

    Surah Al-Falaq (113)
    قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ۝ مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ ۝ وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ

    Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak from the evil of that which He created..."

    Surah An-Nas (114)
    قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ ۝ مَلِكِ النَّاسِ ۝ إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ

    Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind, the God of mankind..."

    Durood-e-Ibrahim (Sending Blessings)
    اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

    O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim.

    Everyday Masnoon & Quranic Duas

    1. Dua in Distress & Worry
    لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ

    There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers. (Surah Al-Anbya 21:87)

    2. Dua for Ease & Clarity
    رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي

    My Lord, expand for me my chest [with assurance] and ease for me my task. (Surah Ta-Ha 20:25-26)

    3. Dua for Protection & Trust
    حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ

    Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs. (Surah Ali 'Imran 3:173)

    4. Dua for Parents & Family
    رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

    My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up [when I was] small. (Surah Al-Isra 17:24)

    5. Dua for Knowledge & Guidance
    رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

    My Lord, increase me in knowledge. (Surah Ta-Ha 20:114)

    6. Dua for Good in This World & Hereafter
    رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

    Our Lord, give us in this world [that which is] good and in the Hereafter [that which is] good and protect us from the punishment of the Fire. (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201)
